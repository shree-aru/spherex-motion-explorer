Description of AlgorithmThe algorithm consists of two steps. First, identifying Source Blooming,
Snowballs and other transient events; and second, applying the extended halo (HALO) flag.

Step 2. Non-Overflow Bloom FlaggingIt has also been observed that there are cases where pixels
illuminated by a source exhibit the TRANSIENT flag without also triggering the OVERFLOW flag. This
may be due to sub-pixel pointing jitter, variable star signal or pre-saturation charge repulsion from the
central pixel (also known as the brighter-fatter effect). In these cases, the flux recorded in the transient
pixel is commensurate with a source. Thus, after the identification of Source Blooming and Snowballs, the
algorithm searches the remaining pixels for groups of blooming transients without an OVERFLOW center,
i.e., contiguous pixels containing both EARLY TRANSIENT and LATE TRANSIENT flags according to
the criteria in Table 10, prioritizing the greatest fluence. As high transient images will encourage false posi-
tives, images identified as HIGH TRANS are ignored, as are pixels flagged NONFUNCT and pixels already
flagged BLOOM or SNOWBALL. The identification of Non-Overflow Source Blooming proceeds along the
same lines as for Source Blooming and Snowballs.
